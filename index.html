01About Me

I am a final-year undergraduate in Political Science at Jagannath University, Dhaka, Bangladesh (Bachelor of Social Science, Honours — expected November 2026), and I am applying for Master's programmes for the September 2027 intake.

My research looks at clean-energy transitions in developing economies: green-hydrogen readiness, energy security and low-carbon development; renewable-energy investment and supply chains in South and Southeast Asia; and China–Global South economic relations under the Belt and Road Initiative. I am currently contributing to a research manuscript on green-hydrogen transition readiness and conducting a preregistered systematic review on the political economy of clean-energy transitions.

My wider interests include international political economy, water governance and sustainable development. I am also learning Mandarin Chinese at the Confucius Institute, University of Dhaka.
