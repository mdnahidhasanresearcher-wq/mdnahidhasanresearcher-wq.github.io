About

I am a final-year Bachelor of Social Science (Honours) student in Political Science at Jagannath University, Dhaka, Bangladesh (expected November 2026), and a Master's applicant for the September 2027 intake.

My research sits at the intersection of energy policy and political economy. I study how developing economies prepare for clean-energy transitions, with a focus on green-hydrogen readiness, renewable-energy investment and supply chains, and the role of China's Belt and Road Initiative in financing low-carbon development across the Global South.

Energy Investment, Trade & Global Supply Chains

China's BRI & the Political Economy of Climate and Energy

China–Global South economic relations, low-carbon investment and the governance of clean-energy transitions.

Green Hydrogen Transition Readiness

July 2026 – Present

“Multidimensional Assessment of Green Hydrogen Transition Readiness in Selected South and Southeast Asian Developing Economies”

Supervisor: Dr. Tausif Ali, School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Shandong University of Technology, China
